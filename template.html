What this is

A valley living through the singularity. ~45 residents each decide what to do with Yuka goal evaluators, weighing four loyalties: self family community nation.

It starts as an ordinary present-day town: houses, shops, offices, parking lots, gas stations, a warehouse, a factory. As robots and AI spread, lots go empty and their owners redevelop them: parking becomes solar canopies or gardens, offices become compute or apartments, cars give way to robotaxis.

Solar makes energy, racks turn energy into compute, robots need both and take over work. Appetite for all three grows faster than anyone can build. Every few months a discovery changes the rules.

Whoever owns the engine collects its income. Helix Systems (outside capital, ) owns most of it at the start. The bar under each productive building shows who owns it, and the stake color mode tints every lot by owner: town co-op a household.

You are one resident (the white dot). Propose co-ops, invest, build, call council votes, and talk to neighbors. Ideas spread as people spend time together.
