## Festus, Missouri

- **The town.** A Mississippi River town about 30 minutes south of St. Louis, twinned with Crystal City (the "Twin Cities"). About 57.8% of households own their homes. 27.8% of residents are under 18. Construction (13.7%) and manufacturing (10.5%) are big employers of residents, and no residents work in farming. About a third commute to other Missouri counties, mostly toward St. Louis. [censusreporter](https://censusreporter.org/profiles/16000US2924094-festus-mo/), [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/festuscitymissouri/PST045225)
- **Budget.** Sales tax is about 69% of the General Fund ($7.68M of $11.12M), and voters have rejected a local use tax several times. [Leader FY27 budget](https://www.myleaderpaper.com/news/festus-fy27-budget-project/article_f85ec6b1-5ce8-4840-95ce-b7fa10fed60a.html), [festusmo.gov sales tax](https://www.festusmo.gov/187/Sales-Tax-Breakdown)
- **Government.** Mayor and council: 8 members, 2 per ward, with one seat per ward elected each April. The mayor (Sam Richards) is up in April 2027. [festusmo.gov elections](https://www.festusmo.gov/432/Election-Central)
- **The deal.** CRG (Clayco's data center arm) is building a $6B campus on about 370 acres north of Hwy 67. No operator has been named.
  - **Terms:** no real property abatement; a tiered personal property abatement; $45M in community payments ($3M/yr for years 1–5, $5M/yr for years 6–10) plus up to $5M for a firehouse; buyouts of 11 homes on Glenkee Court plus 1; the developer pays water, sewer and Ameren upgrades; noise capped at 60 dB.
  - **The city's model:** $1.3B over 25 years, with $581M of $803M in taxes to Festus R-VI schools; 300 MW in 2028 and 1 GW by 2031.
  - Sources: [Mayor's statement with terms (PDF)](https://fas.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/City-of-Festus-Public-Statement-from-Mayor-Richards-3202026-with-attachments.pdf), [STLPR approval](https://www.stlpr.org/government-politics-issues/2026-03-31/festus-data-center-development-approval)
- **The fight.**
  - CRG came to Festus after withdrawing a bid in St. Charles ([STLPR](https://www.stlpr.org/government-politics-issues/2025-11-13/developer-eyes-festus-site-new-data-center-development)).
  - The council approved the deal 6–2 on March 30, 2026, after the mayor cut off comment.
  - On April 7, 2026 voters ousted all four incumbents on the ballot. Turnout went from 920 to 2,126. [KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/politics-elections-and-government/2026-04-09/festus-missouri-data-center-election-city-council), [E&E News](https://www.eenews.net/articles/missouri-town-fires-half-its-city-council-over-data-center-deal/)
  - Wake Up JeffCo sued over the rezoning, the deal and Sunshine Law violations ([STLPR](https://www.stlpr.org/government-politics-issues/2026-04-10/data-center-opposition-group-sues-city-festus-proposed-developer)).
  - Recall petitions were certified, then rejected by the council. A judge dismissed the case to force recalls on August 31 ([Leader](https://www.myleaderpaper.com/news/festus-recall-elections-case-dismissed/article_60d2aae4-204f-4816-b37b-bfaebaa69f4f.html)).
  - The summary judgment hearing is December 10, 2026 ([Leader](https://www.myleaderpaper.com/news/wake-up-jeffco-lawsuit-hearing-held-oct-9/article_d7e4a170-8dda-470c-9142-be268172c751.html)).
  - The game's December 2026 "court ruling" event and its April elections come from this.
- **Water.** It comes from the Jefferson County Water Authority (shared with Herculaneum), which draws from Mississippi River **collector wells**: a newer main well with the original kept as backup, plus city wells for emergencies. The original was designed for 4 MGD but reached only about 2 MGD in 2019. The rate is $13.64 per 1,000 gallons. [festusmo.gov water](http://www.festusmo.gov/157/Water-Department), [distribution](https://www.festusmo.gov/165/Distribution)
- **Power.** Ameren Missouri. The state-approved large-load tariff for data centers sets a 12-year minimum contract, 2 years of bills as collateral and exit fees. Ameren has asked to raise rates in mid-2027. [Missouri PSC](https://psc.mo.gov/General/Utility_Tariffs_for_Large_Load_Customers), [Ameren](https://ameren.mediaroom.com/2026-06-26-Storm-hardening,-electric-grid-upgrades-and-new-generation-underpin-request-to-update-electric-rates-in-mid-2027)
- **Memory of industry leaving.**
  - PPG's Crystal City glass plant closed at the end of 1990, idling 273 workers ([UPI](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1990/09/20/PPG-to-close-Missouri-flat-glass-plant/3187653803200/)).
  - Doe Run's Herculaneum lead smelter closed in 2013 after buying out 160 homes ([STLPR](https://www.stlpr.org/show/st-louis-on-the-air/2022-01-21/herculaneums-environmental-cleanup-offers-lessons-for-today)).
- **Unions.** The pipefitters' local (Plumbers & Pipefitters Local 562) backed the deal.

## Ownership and purpose: Shapiro and Mostaque

- **David Shapiro, post-labor economics.**
  - Income comes from wages, capital or transfers. As wages fall, ownership (property income) has to replace them, and transfers are only a floor.
  - His Pyramid of Prosperity stacks: transfers (UBI, basic services); public wealth funds (Norway, Alaska, Chattanooga); collective assets (co-ops, ESOPs, land trusts, data centers, solar farms); personal assets; and whatever wages are left.
  - He lists 16 property-based income streams with real precedents.
  - His 12 Commandments: build enduring funds rather than spending windfalls, and track the **ownership share of income**.
  - Purpose rests on autonomy, competence, relatedness and security. Dividends cover security; the rest has to come from what people do. He calls the result a "meaning economy."
  - Sources: [Post-labor economics update (16 interventions)](https://daveshap.substack.com/p/post-labor-economics-update), [Six easy steps](https://daveshap.substack.com/p/understanding-post-labor-economics), [12 Commandments](https://daveshap.substack.com/p/the-12-commandments-of-post-labor), [Pyramids of Prosperity and Power](https://daveshap.substack.com/p/the-pyramids-of-prosperity-and-power), [Meaning crisis](https://daveshap.substack.com/p/im-going-through-quite-the-meaning)
- **Emad Mostaque, Intelligent Internet (ii.inc).**
  - **The Champion** is a locally owned intelligence utility (agents, integration engineers, humanoids as a service). 10% of its equity goes to everyone under 21, vesting at 21, plus 0.5% to each new birth year, forever. Every person also gets a free AI agent.
  - **Foundation Coins** would be minted only with proof of useful compute ("Proof of Benefit"). They are not live.
  - *The Last Economy* measures health as **Material × Intelligence × Network × Diversity** capital, multiplied together, so a zero in any one sinks the whole.
  - Sources: [The Last Economy (PDF)](https://webstatics.ii.inc/The%20Last%20Economy.pdf), [II-Medical-8B](https://huggingface.co/Intelligent-Internet/II-Medical-8B), [$1 Champions](https://aisocratic.org/news/emad-mostaque-pitches-locally-owned-ai-champions-founded-at-a-1-valuation)
- **Reference points.**
  - The Alaska Permanent Fund Dividend was $1,000 in 2025 ([Alaska DOR](https://dor.alaska.gov/department-of-revenue/news-detail/2025/09/22/department-of-revenue-announces-2025-permanent-fund-dividend-amount)).
  - Norway's fund held NOK 22,014bn at Q3 2026 ([NBIM](https://www.nbim.no/en/news-and-insights/1q-3q/key-figures-q3-2026/)).
  - About 900 electric co-ops serve 42M people ([NRECA](https://www.electric.coop/electric-cooperative-fact-sheet)).
  - Chattanooga's EPB reports $5.3B of community benefit ([EPB](https://epb.com/newsroom/press-releases/2025-community-benefit-study/)).
  - Unemployment causes measurable distress (meta-analysis of 324 studies, [doi](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jvb.2009.01.001)).

**How this shows up in the game:** equity tiers, the Festus Fund and dividend, the kids' trust (Champion youth equity), the compute royalty, the co-op hall, the Purpose meter, and projects built by robots or by neighbors working with robots.
